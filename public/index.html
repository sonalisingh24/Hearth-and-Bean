Our story

Started with a single grinder and a stubborn idea.

Hearth & Bean began as a weekend experiment: what if a café felt less like a stop on the way somewhere and more like the living room you wish you had? We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise, and keep the music low enough for conversation.

Bring a book, a laptop, or a friend you haven't seen in a while. The corner table by the window is the best seat in the house — but it's first come, first served.

  • Small-batch beans sourced from estates we've actually visited.
  • Baked daily — when it's gone, it's gone.
  • No rush — stay as long as your cup (or your story) lasts.
